Global settings. The level lines below are Advanced, the game as balanced. Basic multiplies every level's HP × and damage × by these numbers (the Tutorial ignores them). Shown as dotted lines on both charts. Saved to js/difficulty.js.

Enemy HP × and damage × by level

Each level's enemyDelta. Drag a point up or down (snaps to 0.05). Hold Shift while dragging to move that level and every level after it. Click a point, then use ↑ ↓ (Shift for 0.25). The small number between two points is the step from one level to the next.
