Campaign pressure

Every wave of every level, left to right. Pressure is how hard the wave pushes against the firepower your Tokens can buy, with the Virus tax taken off. Above 1.0 the defence can't keep up and enemies leak. The dashed line is the target curve (edit it in tools/ledger/target.js). Shaded columns are waves with Viruses: the darker, the more of them.

Level summary

One card per level: its pressure (solid) against the target (dashed), and a verdict. Click a card to look at that level below and shape it.

Enemy arrivals

Level. Bars: enemy HP arriving per second, by enemy. Line: the player's firepower if every Token so far went into the build tower, after the Virus tax. Bars above the line mean pressure above 1.0.

Fill rate

How fast the field fills to MAV (the cap on enemy EV alive at once) if nothing dies, against how long your firepower takes to clear a full field. When clearing takes longer than filling, the field piles up: that's the opening punch.

Wave table

Tower value

Damage per second for every 100 Tokens spent, at Tier 1 and fully upgraded to Tier 4 (upgrade costs included).

Damage per enemy pass

Damage one medium-speed enemy takes walking through a tower's range, per 100 Tokens. Range matters here, which is where long reach pays off.

Enemy value

EV is the wave-budget price of an enemy. Threat EV is what its stats say it should cost: HP × speed, scaled so Hack stays at 100 (killing an enemy in one pass needs DPS ≈ HP × speed ÷ reach). Abilities aren't in the formula yet; the sim will measure them.

How the ledger works

  • Tower DPS follows the game's firing code exactly: one clip unit per trigger pull (a whole burst), reload after the clip's last pull, no cooldown during reload.
  • Enemy inflow replays the wave spawner's random picks 400 times per wave and averages them. It treats the field as empty, so MAV never throttles spawning; in real play MAV only slows things when you're already behind.
  • Player Tokens are the level's start plus every kill reward from earlier waves, as if every enemy died and every Token went into the build tower at Tier 1.
  • Pressure = the worst of three parts (tick "Show the parts" to see them): throughput, tank and burst, all measured against firepower after the Virus tax.
  • Throughput pressure = enemy HP per second ÷ total DPS. HP arrives over the spawn time plus one walk down the board.
  • Tank pressure = can the single toughest enemy (Crack or Worm) walk through? Its HP ÷ the damage it soaks crossing one tower's reach with every tower on it. It spikes the wave a new tank first appears. Tanks aren't what makes TDX hard, so it's only shown with the parts.
  • Burst pressure = a full field of enemies (MAV) ÷ the damage your towers deal while it crosses the board. More entry points split your towers across routes (the "Entry split" assumption). Throughput is the average; burst is the peak, which is what a cold start on a short board feels like.
  • Virus tax = Tokens of towers each Virus costs you: towers it drains plus the Vaults you build to stop it. Measured by the sim's reactive bot (about 110 per Virus at damage 10). Half is taken off your firepower in the wave it arrives, all of it from the next wave on.
  • Target curve lives in tools/ledger/target.js: per level a start and end pressure, a straight climb between, and a dip on the wave after the middle one. The game never reads it.
  • Level summary verdict: the level's average pressure minus the target's average. More than 0.1 below = Too easy, more than 0.1 above = Too hard, otherwise On target. "Off by" is the average distance from the curve, wave by wave, so a level can be on target on average but the wrong shape.
  • Shaping dials change one level's waves in the ledger only: Virus first wave moves where Viruses join the roster (they stay in every wave after); Last wave size ramps TEV from ×1 at wave 1 up to that at the last wave; Start Tokens and Wave 1 cap (MAV) scale those values. The box under the dials says what to change in config.js to make it real.
  • Calibration: how the ledger models a player. Build tower, grenade and pellet hit rates, tower distance and maze factor are hand guesses; Virus tax and Entry split were measured with the sim (E13, E7).
  • Fill: time to fill = MAV ÷ EV spawned per second. Full-field HP = the HP that MAV's worth of this wave's mix carries. Time to clear = full-field HP ÷ your DPS at that wave. A wave whose whole budget is below MAV never fills.
  • Threat EV = HP × speed × (Hack EV ÷ Hack HP × speed). Price ratio = EV ÷ Threat EV: below 1 is underpriced (more threat than the budget charges), above 1 overpriced. Tokens per 100 threat shows how generously each enemy pays for the trouble it causes.
  • Not modelled: board layout beyond row count and entry points, CRASH, the Virus spiral (the tax is flat; losing towers compounds in play), Bug damage, overkill and targeting waste, unlock order, selling. These are the sim's job (tools/sim/).
