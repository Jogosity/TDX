Intensity

Bars are each wave's enemy budget (TEV), with the field cap (MAV) as a tick. Drag the three big handles to bend the curve; every wave follows it. Drag a wave's own dot to pin it by hand (amber); double-click a pin to let it follow the curve again. Click a bar to add or remove a Breach Surge (red: extra enemies that pour from every entry point mid-wave).

Ledger pressure

How hard each wave pushes against the firepower your Tokens can buy (1.0 = enemies start to leak). Solid = this level now, dashed = your target: drag its two ends. Shaded columns have Viruses.

Roster

Drag an enemy onto a wave to add it from there. Drag a bar to move it, drag its ends to change when it joins and leaves. Weight makes an enemy more (or less) likely than the others when a spawn is picked. Numbers in the bars are the Ledger's expected count per wave.
